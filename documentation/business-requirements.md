# Business Requirements

## Objective

Create an executive analytics solution that consolidates business performance into a clear, decision-ready reporting experience.

## Core Reporting Requirements

### Must Have
- Executive KPI overview
- Current-period performance
- Previous-period comparison
- Trend analysis
- Filters and slicers
- Drill-down capability
- Validated business definitions
- Consistent date logic

### Nice to Have
- Segment analysis
- Variance explanations
- Drill-through pages
- Exception indicators
- Dynamic titles and context-aware labels

### Excluded
- Confidential employer or client data
- Personally identifiable information
- Credentials and connection strings
- Proprietary source-system details

## Validation

Measures and visuals should be reconciled against source totals and validated against agreed business rules before publication.
